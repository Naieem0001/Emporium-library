Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about Emporium Library.

How do I download books from Emporium Library?
Open any book card on the home page or a genre page, click the book to view its details and use the Download button. All downloads are free and require no subscription.
Are all books on Emporium Library free?
Yes. Every ebook and every study note on Emporium Library is completely free to read and download. There are no paywalls and no hidden charges.
How do I access the Academic Hub?
Click Academic Hub in the top navigation or visit /academic.html directly. You can browse and download student notes without an account.
How can I upload my own study notes and earn points?
Go to the Academic Hub, sign in, and use the upload form. Each approved upload earns you 5 contributor points, and top contributors get featured on the platform.
Do I need an account to read books?
No. You can browse, read and download books without any account. An account is only required to upload content to the Academic Hub or track your contributor points.
